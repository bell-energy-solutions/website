Let's build your website

This page gets you set up in about 15 minutes. After that, Claude does almost all the work and shows you pictures whenever it needs your help.

What you'll need

  • A Mac or Windows computer
  • A paid Claude plan that includes Claude Code (for example Pro or Max). Check the current plans at claude.com/pricing.
  • The login for the company where you bought your web address (for example GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Squarespace Domains)
  • Your business email inbox, to confirm the new accounts Claude helps you set up
  • Your phone, for security codes
  • A payment card for a small pay-as-you-go charge from Anthropic, so Claude can make changes for you on GitHub later. You choose a monthly limit, and Claude explains it when you get there.
  • Some photos of your business (you can add these later)
It takes about 2–3 hours of your time in total, spread over a few days. You can stop whenever you like. Claude keeps notes in your folder, so it picks up where you left off.
1

Install the Claude app

Go to claude.ai/download, download the app for your computer, open it, and sign in.

✔ You'll know it worked when you see the Claude app open with a message box.

Already have it? Make sure it's up to date, then go to step 2.

2

Make a folder for your website

On your Desktop, make a new folder and name it My Website.

  • Mac: right-click an empty spot on the Desktop and choose New Folder.
  • Windows: right-click an empty spot on the Desktop and choose New → Folder.

Then drag the two files you were given, SITE_BUILDER.md and this page (START_HERE.html), into that folder.

On Windows the names may show without the ending, as just SITE_BUILDER and START_HERE. That's normal: they're the same files.

Simplified drawing: your screen will look a little different.

3

Open the Code tab and choose your folder

In the Claude app, click Code (near the top or side of the window). Then click the folder picker near the message box and choose your My Website folder.

Simplified drawing: your screen will look a little different. The official guide with real pictures is at code.claude.com/docs/en/desktop.

4

Two quick settings

Near the message box there are small menus.

  • Model: if you see a model menu, choose Opus (the newest one listed).
  • Mode: if you see a mode menu, choose Auto. If there's no Auto, choose Accept edits.
Please don't choose "Bypass permissions". It turns off safety checks.

Not sure? That's fine. Just go to step 5 and Claude will help you set these.

5

Tell Claude to start

Copy this sentence, paste it into the message box, and press Enter:

Read SITE_BUILDER.md and run it.

✔ You'll know it worked when Claude replies and starts walking you through the setup.

What to expect

Permission boxes. Sometimes a small box will ask if Claude may do something on your computer, like this:

DenyAllow

Read what the box says it's for. If you're ever unsure, click Deny and ask Claude, "What was that for?"

Questions with buttons. Claude will often ask you something with clickable answers, like "Done, it worked" or "I'm stuck". Just click the one that fits.

Picture guides. When Claude needs you to do something (like create an account), it will open a guide page with pictures, one step at a time.

Stuck? Take a screenshot and drag it into the message box. Claude can see it and will help.

Three golden rules

  1. Never type a password into Claude. You'll always log in yourself, in your web browser.
  2. It's fine to say "I don't understand." Claude will explain it differently.
  3. You're in charge. Nothing goes on your website until you've checked it and said yes.
